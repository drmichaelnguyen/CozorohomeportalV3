# Customer Support Knowledge Template

This folder is the safe starting point for customer-facing bot knowledge.

Add only approved content here, such as:

- housing policy
- cancellation policy
- discount rules
- coins policy
- laundry rules
- support hours

Do not place:

- passwords or secrets
- internal operations notes
- private staff procedures
- raw resident data

The bot will answer only as well as the approved documents it can retrieve.
